How to read this report

  • Confirmed cash currently includes paid featured Business Directory listings only.
  • Marketplace commission is a receivable calculated from completed service requests.
  • Affiliate commission is payable when referrals are converted and approved; paid referrals are shown separately.
  • Advertising budgets, event budgets and communications plan value are pipeline indicators, not collected money.
  • Net profit is not available until operating costs, Paystack charges and settlement expenses are recorded.

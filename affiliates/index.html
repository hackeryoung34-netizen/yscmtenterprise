Refer customers to YSCMT Enterprise products and services. Approved partners receive a unique referral code, and eligible commissions are reviewed and approved before payout.

How the programme works

  1. Apply with your contact details and promotion channels.
  2. YSCMT reviews your application.
  3. Approved partners receive a unique referral link.
  4. Eligible referrals are tracked and reviewed.
  5. Commissions are confirmed and paid under the partner agreement.

Commission rates, eligible products, attribution windows and payout terms are confirmed in writing when your application is approved. Submitting an application does not guarantee acceptance or earnings.

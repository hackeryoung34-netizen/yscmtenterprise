Community Guidelines

YSCMT Connect is a space for students, teachers, mentors and creators to learn from one another. Help us keep it welcoming, useful and safe.

Be respectful

Treat members with dignity. Disagreement is welcome; harassment, threats, hate speech, bullying and targeted abuse are not.

Share responsibly

Post content you have the right to share. Credit creators. Do not publish private information, confidential documents, impersonation, scams, malware or misleading promotions.

Keep conversations safe

Do not pressure members for money, passwords, verification codes or sensitive personal information. Be careful when opening links or joining external meeting rooms.

Protect privacy

Respect profile visibility and post audiences. Private messages are intended for their participants; do not share another person's private conversation without permission, except where required to report a safety concern.

Report harmful content

Use the report control on a post or contact yscmtenterprise@gmail.com if you see abuse, fraud, impersonation or unsafe material. Reports may be reviewed by YSCMT moderators, who can hide or remove content.

Enforcement

Depending on severity and repeated behaviour, YSCMT may remove content, restrict visibility, suspend access or close an account. We aim to act proportionately and protect the community.

These guidelines may be updated as the community grows.

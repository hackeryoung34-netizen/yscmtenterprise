Privacy & Safety

This page explains the information YSCMT Connect uses to provide its community features.

Information you provide

Your account uses your sign-in email. Your profile may include your display name, username, country, interests, biography and profile photo. You choose whether your profile is public or private.

Community activity

When you use the service, we store posts, comments, likes, follows, group memberships, stories, messages, live-session details, reports and related timestamps so the features work and safety issues can be reviewed.

Who can see your content

Public posts and public profiles can be seen by signed-in community members. Posts shared with followers are limited to the intended audience. Private profiles are excluded from member discovery. Direct messages are returned only to members of that conversation through the app.

Photos, videos and stories

Uploaded media is stored in private object storage. The app issues temporary access links when displaying media. Stories are designed to expire after 24 hours and are automatically cleaned up by a scheduled task; backups or operational logs may retain limited records for a period.

Safety and moderation

Members can report content and block other members. YSCMT moderators may review reports and hide or remove content that violates the Community Guidelines.

Retention and requests

We retain information for as long as needed to operate the service, protect members and meet legitimate recordkeeping needs. To request help with your account or personal information, email yscmtenterprise@gmail.com.

Security

We use authenticated routes, access checks and private media storage. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Do not share passwords, one-time codes or highly sensitive information in community posts or messages.

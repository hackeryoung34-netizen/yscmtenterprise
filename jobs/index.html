How the YSCMT job board works

The YSCMT job board helps job seekers discover vacancies submitted by employers across Nigeria. Search by keyword, category, state or employment type. Job adverts are reviewed before publication, and only approved vacancies appear in search results.

For job seekers

Read the full description, confirm the employer independently and use the application details supplied in the advert. Check the closing date and location before applying.

For employers

Submit a clear job title, employer name, responsibilities, location and application method. Your advert remains pending until YSCMT reviews it.

The board covers opportunities in all 36 Nigerian states and the Federal Capital Territory, subject to vacancies being submitted and approved. Browse YSCMT technology services or explore the Nigerian business directory.
